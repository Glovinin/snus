## Overview

SnusIdea is a complete marketplace platform in the style of Shopify/Amazon, **based in Portugal**, that allows sellers to create and manage their own online stores, selling snus and nicotine products to **all of Europe and internationally**.

**Market Context:**
- **Origin**: Portugal
- **Primary Market**: European Union (27 countries)
- **Secondary Market**: Rest of the world (Americas, Asia, Africa, Oceania)
- **Primary Currency**: EUR (Euro)
- **Primary Language**: Portuguese (PT-PT)
- **Regulation**: GDPR (Europe), international local legislations
- **Launch Date**: December 22, 2024

**Timeline:**
- **MVP Development**: Ongoing until December 22, 2024
- **Testing and QA**: November - December 2024
- **MVP Launch**: December 22, 2024 - **Functional MVP ready to sell**
- **Post-Launch**: Continuous development with best-in-class market technologies
- **Maintenance**: Daily maintenance and updates

**Development Philosophy:**
- **December 22, 2024**: Launch of functional MVP ready for sales
- **Continuous Development**: Ongoing development with cutting-edge technologies
- **Daily Maintenance**: Regular updates, bug fixes, and performance improvements
- **Technology Stack**: Best-in-class technologies from the market
- **Scalability**: Platform designed to grow and evolve continuously

This documentation details all the functionalities necessary to operate a world-class e-commerce platform with global reach, with special focus on the needs of the European and international market. **The MVP with core functionalities will be launched on December 22, 2024, ready for sales, with continuous development and daily maintenance thereafter.**

## Nicotine Product Regulations (Europe)

### 22.1 Compliance with European Legislation

**TPD (Tobacco Products Directive) - Directive 2014/40/EU:**
- **Regulation**: Applicable to all tobacco and nicotine products in EU
- **Pre-market notification**: Mandatory for new products
- **Nicotine limits**: Maximum 20mg/ml per portion
- **Health warnings**: Mandatory on packaging
- **Marketing restrictions**: Limitations on advertising and promotions
- **Traceability**: Product tracking system

**Age Verification (18+):**
- **Mandatory verification** at checkout
- Age validation by:
  - Date of birth
  - Identity verification (optional for high-value orders)
  - Age declaration
- Automatic blocking of minors
- "Know Your Customer" (KYC) policy

**Restrictions by Country:**
- **Sweden**: Snus allowed (only EU country)
- **Norway**: Snus allowed (not EU member)
- **Other EU countries**: Traditional snus sale prohibited, but nicotine pouches allowed
- **United Kingdom**: Snus and nicotine pouches allowed (post-Brexit)
- Automatic eligibility verification by delivery country

**Documentation and Tracking:**
- Product registration according to TPD
- Compliance certificates
- Complete supply chain traceability
- Periodic reports to competent authorities

**Packaging and Labeling:**
- Mandatory health warnings (30% of packaging)
- Ingredient information
- Nicotine content clearly indicated
- Expiration date
- Usage instructions
- Compliance with packaging guidelines

**Shipping Restrictions:**
- Destination country verification before shipping
- Automatic blocking of shipments to countries where product is prohibited
- Adequate customs documentation
- Accurate content declaration
